# Paint the Town — store copy

## Short description (300 chars max)

Paintball across nine Toronto landmarks! Battle 19 cartoon animal rivals from
the Royal Ontario Museum to the Zoo, find each Portal Key, and light the exit
beam. A colourful retro FPS campaign on the classic DOOM engine — no gore,
all paint.

## Long description ("About This Game")

**Nine landmarks. One colourful city.**

Toronto's portals have gone dark, and it's up to you — and your paintball
marker — to bring the colour back. Fight through faithful retro renditions of
the Royal Ontario Museum, Queen's Park, Sankofa Square, Nathan Phillips
Square, Union Station, the hockey arena, the ballpark, the Toronto Islands
and the Toronto Zoo.

- **19 original animal rivals** — raccoon gunners, armoured pigeons, transit
  drones, a museum dinosaur skeleton and more, each with venue-appropriate
  tactics
- **Three paintball markers** — starter, pump and automatic, sharing one
  paint supply
- **Living venues** — hockey faceoffs, synchronized baseball, street vendors,
  zoo wildlife with real front/side/rear views, wandering visitors and
  cheering crowds
- **Keyed portal exits** — find the octagonal Portal Key, press USE at the
  gold panel, and walk into the beam
- **Nine original mellow soundtrack pieces** and a hand-built HUD, automap
  and campaign map
- **Classic engine, modern comfort** — runs on the bundled Woof! source port
  (GPL) with the included Freedoom-based assets; fully self-contained

Family-friendly: paint splats instead of blood, PG-13 throughout.
